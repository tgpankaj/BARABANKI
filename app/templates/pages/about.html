About Barabanki District & Barabanki Connect

Bridging centuries of cultural harmony, handloom craftsmanship, and agricultural abundance with digital access, education, and civic empowerment.

District Profile: Barabanki, Uttar Pradesh

Situated in the heart of the Awadh region in central Uttar Pradesh, District Barabanki is bounded by the state capital Lucknow to the west, Sitapur to the north, Bahraich and Gonda across the sacred Ghaghara river to the northeast, and Ayodhya and Amethi to the east and south.

Comprising six tehsils—Nawabganj (District Headquarters), Fatehpur, Ramnagar, Sirauli Ghauspur, Haidergarh, and Rudauli—Barabanki is historically celebrated for its syncretic cultural traditions where people of diverse faiths celebrate shared spiritual values.

Cultural & Historical Heritage

Deva Sharif & Sufi Harmony

The revered shrine of 19th-century Sufi mystic Hazrat Haji Waris Ali Shah is an international symbol of peace and unconditional love, celebrated with the motto "Jo Rab Hai Wahi Ram Hai" (The God of one is the God of all). The annual Deva Mela attracts millions with its All-India Mushaira and traditional exhibitions.

Parijaat Tree of Kintoor

Located in village Kintoor, named after Queen Kunti from the Mahabharata, the celestial Parijaat Tree is a rare protected botanical wonder whose fragrant blossoms open only under the moonlight.

Handloom Legacy of Zaidpur

Barabanki's weaving cluster at Zaidpur is world-famous for its master Jacquard weavers, who weave fine cotton, linen, and silk scarves, dupattas, and stoles supplied to leading domestic fashion houses and export markets.

Our Mission: Barabanki Connect

Barabanki Connect was conceived and developed by a dedicated B.Tech IT student with a clear objective: to give Barabanki an authentic, high-speed, accessible digital civic hub.

  • Empower Local Commerce: Provide free and verified digital listings for shops, textile collectives, and service providers.
  • Support Local Youth & Students: Aggregate job vacancies, apprenticeships, college details, and technical study groups.
  • Transparent Civic Information: Attribute official news bulletins to accurate public sources without sensationalism.
  • Community Moderation: Foster constructive, respectful public forums where civic issues can be raised and addressed constructively.
