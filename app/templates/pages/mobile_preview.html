📱 Mobile Design System

Barabanki Connect is designed mobile-first for the local community, ensuring smooth operation even on entry-level Android devices and 3G/4G connectivity.

  • Sticky Bottom App Bar: 5 primary navigation anchors with active tactile feedback.
  • Touch-Friendly Hit Targets: All buttons and interactive cards meet the 48x48px WCAG criterion.
  • Zero Horizontal Overflow: Rigid viewport constraint with fluid responsive flex and grid.
  • Fast Lightweight SVG Graphics: Instant rendering without heavy imagery dependencies.
