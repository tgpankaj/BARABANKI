Advertising Policies & Guidelines

Transparent standards designed to protect Barabanki residents while empowering legitimate local commerce.

1. General Principles

Barabanki Connect is a community and civic platform. Advertising must enhance the resident experience rather than degrade it. All advertising placements are clearly distinguished with an "Ad" or "Sponsored" label and are never confused with editorial recommendations.

  • Advertisers must represent registered or verified local enterprises operating within District Barabanki or neighboring areas.
  • We enforce maximum frequency caps (no more than 1–2 sponsored cards per view) to prevent user fatigue.
  • Zero fake metrics: all impression and click counts are measured internally with deduplication.

2. Prohibited Products and Services

Campaigns promoting any of the following categories are strictly forbidden and will be rejected immediately:

✕ Gambling & Betting: Satta, Matka, sports betting apps, lotteries, and unregulated online casinos.
✕ Counterfeit Goods: Unauthorized replicas, knock-off apparel, and pirated media.
✕ Deceptive Financials: Guaranteed double-money schemes, predatory quick loans, and unauthorized chit funds.
✕ Illicit Substances & Weapons: Tobacco, narcotics, uncertified herbal tonics, firearms, and explosives.

3. Moderation and Approval Process

Every campaign is reviewed by human moderators within 24 business hours. Moderators verify:

  1. That the business is legitimately registered on the portal and active.
  2. That the destination URL is safe, active, and contains no phishing or open redirects.
  3. That the headline, banner, and copy accurately represent the product or service offered.

4. Pricing, Payments & Refund Policy

Transparent Pricing: All packages are fixed-cost with zero hidden platform charges. Pricing is calculated upfront based on selected duration.

Manual Verification: Payments are verified via official bank/UPI UTR reference by district administrators. We never store credit card numbers or process unverified transactions.

Cancellations & Refunds:

  • If a campaign is rejected by moderators, 100% of the verified amount will be returned within 3–5 working days.
  • If an advertiser requests cancellation before the start date, a full refund is granted.
  • Once a campaign is active, advertisers may pause display at any time. Unused days may be rescheduled upon request to administration.

5. Public Reporting & Consumer Safety

Residents who encounter misleading pricing, broken links, or fraudulent offers can report any ad using the public report button. Ads receiving 3 or more independent citizen complaints are immediately flagged for administrative review and temporary suspension.
