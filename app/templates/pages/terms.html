Terms of Service

Last Updated: October 2026 | Barabanki Connect Civic Platform

1. Community Conduct & Guidelines

Barabanki Connect is designed to foster constructive civic interaction. Users agree to refrain from posting:

  • Hate speech, defamatory comments, or harassment targeting any community or individual.
  • Unverified rumors, fraudulent business solicitations, or misleading job vacancy claims.
  • Spam or commercial bot links.

2. Content Attribution & Demonstration Policy

In accordance with our Real Data Policy, news articles and public notices reflect official communications and external citations. Demonstration entries created during platform testing are transparently marked with a "Demo" badge.

3. Content Moderation

Administrators and moderators reserve the right to review, edit, or remove content violating platform rules or community harmony. Repeated violations may result in account termination.
